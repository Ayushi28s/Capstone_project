## Overall Market Signals (Q3 2026, vs. Q2 2026)
- Industry-wide outdoor apparel demand up ~8% YoY, driven by continued post-pandemic
  outdoor recreation growth.
- Discounting frequency across the category up industry-wide from Q2 to Q3, suggesting
  inventory pressure heading into the season — most visible in TrailForge's outerwear and
  base-layer moves.
- Sustainability/durability messaging resonating more with the premium segment (SummitWear
  held every price point across both quarters); price competition concentrated in budget
  and mid-tier segments.
- Budget-tier competitors (ValueTrail-type positioning) gaining disproportionate share
  among first-time outdoor gear buyers under 30 — though their accessories and base-layer
  lines use different materials/capacities than NorthPeak's, so price comparisons in those
  two categories need that caveat, unlike outerwear and footwear where the comparison is
  closer to apples-to-apples.
