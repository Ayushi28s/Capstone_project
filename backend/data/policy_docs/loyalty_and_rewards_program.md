# Loyalty & Rewards Program (NorthPeak Trailhead Rewards)

## Program structure
Three tiers, based on trailing-12-month spend: **Trailhead** (entry, $0+), **Basecamp**
($500+), and **Summit** ($1,500+). Tier is recalculated monthly; a customer who drops below
a tier's threshold has a 60-day grace period before downgrading.

## Earning rate
1 point per $1 spent at the Trailhead tier, 1.25 points per $1 at Basecamp, 1.5 points per
$1 at Summit. Points post 7 days after delivery confirmation (not at time of purchase), to
account for the standard return window.

## Redemption
100 points = $5 in redeemable credit, applied at checkout. Points do not expire as long as
the account has at least one order every 18 months. Redeemed credit is non-refundable if the
associated order is later returned — the points are restored to the account instead, not
paid out as cash.

## Tier benefits beyond earning rate
- **Basecamp**: free standard shipping on all orders, early access to seasonal sales 24
  hours before public launch.
- **Summit**: free standard shipping, 48-hour early access, a dedicated support line
  (skip the standard queue), and one free annual gear inspection/tune-up service.

## How this interacts with refunds and support
When a support agent processes a refund, any points earned on that specific order should be
reversed proportionally — full refund reverses full points, partial refund reverses points
proportional to the refunded amount. This is separate from the standard refund-approval
threshold in the Returns & Refund Policy; point reversal happens automatically and does not
itself require additional approval, even when the underlying refund does.

## Common support scenarios
- **"My points didn't post"**: check the order's delivery date — if it's within the last 7
  days, this is expected and not an error.
- **"I was downgraded a tier"**: confirm whether the 60-day grace period has actually
  elapsed; if not, this is a system error and should be escalated per the Employee
  Escalation Procedures.
- **Points/credit disputes over $100 in value**: treat with the same escalation discipline
  as a billing dispute, even though it's not a cash refund.
