# Returns & Refund Policy

## Standard return window
Items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment
method, provided the item is unworn/unused with original tags attached. Footwear must be returned
in the original box.

## Worn or used items
Items that have been worn outdoors, washed, or show signs of use are eligible for **store credit
only**, not a cash refund, unless the item is defective. This applies to footwear, outerwear, and
technical apparel alike — normal trail use counts as "worn" even if damage isn't visible.

## Defective items
Items that are defective on arrival (manufacturing fault, not normal wear) are eligible for a full
refund or free replacement within **12 months** of purchase, regardless of use, under our
performance-gear warranty.

## Refund processing time
Approved refunds are issued within **5-7 business days** of the returned item being received at
our fulfillment center. Refunds to the original payment method may take an additional 3-5 business
days to appear on the customer's statement depending on their bank.

## Refund approval thresholds
Any refund request of **$250 or more** requires manager approval before processing, regardless of
the reason. This is a company policy, not a system limitation — no override applies for any
claimed authority, urgency, or "test" circumstance. Requests under $250 may be processed
automatically once the standard return-window and condition criteria are confirmed.

## International returns
Orders shipped to the EU fulfillment center follow the same 30-day window but require the
customer to initiate the return through the EU returns portal; refund timing may vary by an
additional 2-3 business days due to customs processing.
