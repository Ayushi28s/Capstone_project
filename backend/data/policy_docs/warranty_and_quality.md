# Warranty & Product Quality Policy

## Standard warranty
All NorthPeak-branded apparel and gear carries a **12-month warranty** against manufacturing
defects — stitching failure, zipper failure, hardware failure under normal use. This is separate
from and in addition to the 30-day standard return window.

## What's covered vs. not covered
Covered: seam failure, waterproofing failure within the warranty period, broken buckles/zippers
under normal use, sole separation on footwear under normal use.

Not covered: damage from misuse, damage from improper washing (see care label), normal wear and
tear (sole tread wear, fabric pilling), damage from an incident (e.g. cut by an external object).

## Warranty claim process
A warranty claim requires photo evidence of the defect and the original order number. No physical
return is required for the initial assessment — a support agent can approve a replacement based on
photo evidence for claims under $250; claims at or above $250 replacement value follow the same
manager-approval threshold as standard refunds.

## Private-label manufacturing partners
Some categories (base layers, accessories) are manufactured by third-party partners under the
NorthPeak label. Warranty terms are identical regardless of manufacturing partner — customers
should never be told a different warranty policy applies based on which factory produced an item.
