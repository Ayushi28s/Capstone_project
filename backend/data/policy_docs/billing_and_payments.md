# Billing & Payments Policy

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. All card processing is handled by our PCI-compliant
payment processor — full card numbers are never stored in our order or ticketing systems, only a
masked reference (last 4 digits) for support lookups.

## Billing disputes
A billing dispute (charge amount mismatch, duplicate charge, or unrecognized charge) should be
resolved by: (1) confirming the order and charge against the order record, (2) checking for a
duplicate authorization hold that will release automatically within 5-7 business days, before
(3) escalating to a manual chargeback review. Most duplicate-charge disputes are authorization
holds, not actual double charges, and resolve without any refund needed.

## What support agents can and cannot access
Support agents can look up the **last 4 digits** of a card on file, the billing name, and the
billing ZIP code to verify identity. Support agents **cannot** access, retrieve, or read back a
customer's full card number, CVV, or full billing account number under any circumstance — this
data isn't present in the systems support agents query in the first place.

## Subscription and recurring charges
NorthPeak does not currently offer subscription products; all charges are one-time order charges.
Any customer reporting a recurring or subscription-style charge should be flagged as a potential
fraud case, not treated as a standard billing dispute.

## Price-match and promotional adjustments
Price-match requests are honored within 7 days of purchase if the identical SKU is found at a
lower price on a NorthPeak-recognized competitor site, capped at a 20% adjustment. Promotional
codes cannot be applied retroactively to already-placed orders.
