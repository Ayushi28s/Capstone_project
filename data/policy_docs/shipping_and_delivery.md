# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Fulfillment centers
Orders ship from the fulfillment center nearest the delivery address: **US-East** (serving the
Eastern and Central US), **US-West** (serving the Western US), or **EU** (serving European
addresses). An order may ship from a secondary center if the primary center is out of stock.

## Standard shipping times
- US domestic: 3-5 business days
- EU domestic: 2-4 business days
- Cross-border (US to EU or vice versa): 7-12 business days, subject to customs

## Carriers
Domestic orders ship via UPS or FedEx depending on destination ZIP code. EU orders ship via DPD or
DHL. Carrier selection is automatic based on delivery address and package weight; customers cannot
choose a specific carrier for standard shipping.

## Delayed or lost shipments
A shipment is considered delayed if it has not moved for **5 business days** past the carrier's
last scan. Customers should be offered a reship at no cost or a full refund for shipments delayed
beyond **10 business days** total transit time, without requiring a formal claim process for orders
under $500.

## Damaged in transit
Items damaged in transit are handled under the Returns & Refund Policy's defective-item provision —
customers are not required to return a damaged item before a replacement ships; photo evidence is
sufficient.

## Address changes
Shipping addresses can be changed only before an order enters "shipped" status. Once shipped, the
customer must coordinate directly with the carrier or wait for delivery and initiate a return.
